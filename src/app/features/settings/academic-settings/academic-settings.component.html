Programs

Courses of study a student is admitted to, such as Science or Business.

Levels

The yearly steps a student moves through, such as Grade 1 to Grade 12.

Classes

The group a student is taught in day to day.

Sections

Parallel streams within one class, such as 1-A and 1-B. Available only while classes are in use.

Subjects

What is taught, and who teaches it. Some schools call these courses.

Terms and semesters

Divides the academic year into reporting periods.

Rooms

Physical spaces a class can be timetabled into.
